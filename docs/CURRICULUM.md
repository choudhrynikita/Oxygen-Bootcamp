## Unlock

Day 1 is open. Day N opens when day N−1 is `lab_done` or `complete`. Boss days do not block the next week if the Friday lab is done. Weekend review may unlock after Friday lab.

## Done rule for a day

A day is `complete` only when the lab is done, the check (quiz) meets the threshold, and the Lock block field note is stored. `lab_done` is enough to unlock tomorrow.
