# Pedagogy

You cannot ask how an apple tastes before you have shown someone an apple.

A total fresher has never opened Oxygen. They do not know XML, DITA, maps, keys, AEM, or Guides. The course is a 90-day desk: they were hired to write a small shop’s handbook. You sit with them.

## The apple, in order

1. A help page is a file. Oxygen is the app that opens it.
2. Type a sentence. Save. Find the file on disk.
3. The same file has a page view and a tags view.
4. Make a new file from a template. Put it in a folder, not on the desktop.
5. Three kinds of pages: what it is, how to do it, facts you look up.
6. A booklet is a table of contents that points at those files.
7. Then reuse, tools, publish, teammates.
8. Adobe’s website tool (AEM) is a different job. It starts in week 9. Not day 1.

## Weeks

| Week | Days | Theme | The apple this week |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–7 | Sit down and write | The app opens. A page saves. A red error can be fixed. |
| 2 | 8–14 | Three kinds of pages | Concept, task, reference — as files, not as a booklet yet. |
| 3 | 15–21 | Make a booklet | A map is a table of contents. Completeness means the links work. |
| 4 | 22–28 | Stop copying the same words | Keys and reuse. |
| 5 | 29–35 | Tools on the desk | Conditions, comments, find/replace. |
| 6 | 36–42 | Make a site or a PDF | Publish from the booklet. Read the log. |
| 7 | 43–49 | Work with other people | Browser Oxygen, review, one place the files live. |
| 8 | 50–55 | When things break | Slow maps, house rules, a booklet you can repair. |
| 9 | 56–62 | The website team next door | AEM author vs publish. First time AEM is taught. |
| 10 | 63–69 | Build a web page | Title, text, image, teaser. |
| 11 | 70–75 | Other ways they write in AEM | Fragments, assets, language copies. |
| 12 | 76–90 | Hand work between the two desks | Guides + Oxygen, then a portfolio. |

## Gates

- Day 1 is open.
- Day N opens when day N−1 is `lab_done` or `complete`.
- The first day of the next week opens only when the week boss is `complete` (lab + quiz at least 70% + a short note). Retry the quiz as often as needed. Friday lab alone does **not** open Monday.
- The 5-minute warmup never opens a day.
- A quiz must not mention a word that has not been taught yet.
- Weeks 1–8 do not teach AEM. One line on day 7 is allowed: “Adobe has a website tool. You open it in week 9.”

## Banned early (content audit)

- Days 1–7: no AEM, Guides, Sites, Schematron, DITA-OT, conref, keyref, map, Maps Manager, WebHelp, Fusion, Chemistry, CCMS.
- Days 1–14: no map, topicref, root map, completeness as a map check.
- Days 1–55: no AEM product teaching (the day-7 one-liner is the exception, and it must not be a quiz).
