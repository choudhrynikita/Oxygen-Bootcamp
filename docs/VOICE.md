# Voice

Talk like a senior writer sitting next to a new hire. Not like a slide deck. Not like a model that has read too many manuals.

## Do

- Short sentences. Common words. “You” and “today”.
- Define a new word in the same sentence you first use it.
- Titles say what they will do: “Install Oxygen.” “Write a how-to.”
- Labs happen in the app, or in a labeled mock if the app cannot open.
- Field notes ask what they did, not what they would refuse.
- Quiz options only use words already taught.

## Do not

These are tells. Content audit fails the build if they show up:

- “sits next to”, “what it forbids”, “keep the … honest”
- “clinic, not a ceremony”, “map surgery”, “desk-ready”
- “hostage”, “zero invented”, “if a slide says”
- “wit is allowed”, “the model wins”, “CSS visual editing”
- “Name X and what it forbids”
- “When would you refuse…”
- Riddle titles (“A concept is what it is — not how to do it”)
- Em-dash lecture titles
- Moral language for files (“honest note”, “defend this library”)

## Example

Bad: “Structured authoring sits next to WYSIWYG.”

Good: “Today you install the writing app and look at the window. That is enough.”

Bad: “Name Maps Manager and what it forbids (treating the tree as the filesystem).”

Good: “Open the booklet view and add one page to it.”
